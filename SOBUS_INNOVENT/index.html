About Us

What is Innovent?

INNOVENT is a dynamic innovation platform that transforms ideas into impactful solutions by bringing together students, innovators, researchers, startups, mentors, and industry experts to address real-world challenges through technology and creative problem-solving.

INNOVENT offers a structured journey from idea to innovation through concept presentations, expert-led masterclasses, mentorship, and innovation showcases. It encourages participants from diverse campuses to develop solutions using emerging technologies such as robotics, drones, IoT, electronics, cybersecurity, and sustainable engineering, empowering them to build prototypes and scalable innovations that create meaningful societal impact.
